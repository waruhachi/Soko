## About

Soko brings the iOS 26 widget layout to your Lock Screen on iOS 16-18. Your widgets sit at the bottom of the screen, just above the flashlight and camera buttons, instead of under the clock.

## Features

- Customize a Lock Screen and swipe the widget row down to move it to the bottom, or up to put it back under the clock.
- Each Lock Screen remembers its own layout, so you can keep different layouts on different wallpapers.
- Widgets line up with the flashlight and camera buttons, just like on iOS 26.
- Notifications stay above your widgets, so nothing gets covered.
- See the widget row where it will appear while you customize.
